## 🚀 Getting Started

### What is the Universal Proposal System?

The Universal Proposal System is an AI-powered tool that helps you create professional proposals for any service or product. It works across all modules (WMS, TMS, Marketplace, MAAS, etc.) and provides:

- **AI-powered insights** to improve your win rate
- **Automatic content generation** based on your needs
- **Win probability analysis** to help you focus on high-probability deals
- **Real-time collaboration** with your team
- **Professional exports** in multiple formats

### Your First Proposal

**Step 1: Navigate to Proposal Builder**
- Click on "Proposals" in the main menu
- Select "Universal Intelligent Builder"
- Or go directly to: `/proposals/universal/new`

**Step 2: Fill in Basic Information**
- **Proposal Title**: Enter a clear, descriptive title
- **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's name
- **Valid Until**: Set the proposal expiration date (default: 30 days)

**Step 3: Review AI Insights**
- As you type, AI insights appear automatically
- These insights help improve your win probability
- Pay attention to critical and high-priority insights

**Step 4: Generate Your Proposal**
- Click "Generate Proposal" button
- The system creates a complete proposal with:
  - All necessary sections
  - AI-enhanced content
  - Win strategy analysis
  - Recommendations

**Step 5: Review and Customize**
- Review the generated proposal
- Customize sections as needed
- Add media, interactive elements, or additional content

**Step 6: Collaborate**
- Invite team members to review
- Add comments and suggestions
- Get feedback before sending

**Step 7: Export and Send**
- Export in your preferred format (PDF, DOCX, etc.)
- Send to the customer
- Track engagement and responses

## 💡 Understanding AI Insights

### Insight Types

1. **WIN_RATE Insights**
   - Shows historical success patterns
   - Helps you understand what works
   - Example: "Similar proposals have 75% win rate"

2. **CONTENT Insights**
   - Recommendations for better content
   - Section suggestions
   - Example: "Add journey analysis section - increases win rate by 20%"

3. **PRICING Insights**
   - Competitive pricing analysis
   - Price range recommendations
   - Example: "Your pricing is 15% above market average"

4. **TIMING Insights**
   - Best send time recommendations
   - Urgency assessment
   - Example: "Response time under 24 hours improves acceptance by 10%"

5. **COMPETITIVE Insights**
   - Your competitive advantages
   - Differentiation strategies
   - Example: "Your 98% on-time delivery is a key differentiator"

6. **RISK Insights**
   - Potential risks identified
   - Mitigation strategies
   - Example: "Pricing may be too high for this customer segment"

7. **OPPORTUNITY Insights**
   - Growth opportunities
   - Value-add suggestions
   - Example: "Include case studies for similar customers"

### Insight Priorities

- **CRITICAL** (Red): Must address immediately
- **HIGH** (Orange): Important, should address
- **MEDIUM** (Yellow): Consider addressing
- **LOW** (Blue): Nice to have

### Acting on Insights

1. **Review all insights** before generating proposal
2. **Prioritize critical and high** priority insights
3. **Implement recommendations** that have high impact
4. **Track results** to learn what works

## 📊 Understanding Win Strategy

### Win Probability

The win probability (0-100%) shows your chances of winning the deal:

- **70%+ (Green)**: High probability - focus on closing
- **50-69% (Yellow)**: Medium probability - work on improvements
- **Below 50% (Red)**: Low probability - significant improvements needed

### Key Strengths

These are your competitive advantages. Highlight them in your proposal:
- Unique capabilities
- Proven track record
- Specialized expertise
- Strong performance metrics

### Recommended Actions

Prioritized actions to improve win probability:
- **Critical**: Must do to have a chance
- **High**: Important for success
- **Medium**: Helpful improvements
- **Low**: Nice to have

### Risk Factors

Potential issues that could hurt your chances:
- Pricing concerns
- Timing issues
- Competitive threats
- Customer fit

**Always address risk factors** with mitigation strategies.
