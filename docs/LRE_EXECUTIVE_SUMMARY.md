## 🎯 **WHAT IS A LEGAL REALITY ENGINE?**

A Legal Reality Engine (LRE) is a comprehensive system that:

1. **Captures Truth** - Records all operational events immutably
2. **Preserves Evidence** - Maintains tamper-evident evidence with integrity proofs
3. **Tracks Obligations** - Monitors contractual, regulatory, and operational obligations
4. **Detects Deviations** - Identifies delays, failures, and non-compliance in real-time
5. **Assesses Liability** - Determines responsibility without blame
6. **Assembles Evidence** - Generates court-ready evidence packets automatically
7. **Ensures Compliance** - Monitors and reports compliance status continuously

**Purpose:** Protect the organization legally while enabling proactive management.
