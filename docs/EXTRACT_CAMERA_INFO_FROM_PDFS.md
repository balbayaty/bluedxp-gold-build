# 📄 How to Extract Camera Information from PDFs

Since the PDFs contain your camera and NVR information, here are several ways to get the data we need:

---

## 🎯 **OPTION 1: Copy-Paste from PDF (Easiest)**

1. **Open the PDF files**:
   - `IP cameras and NVR's 07092023_251220_150249.pdf`
   - `IP cameras and NVR's 07092023_251220_150227.pdf`

2. **Look for these columns/fields**:
   - IP Address
   - Camera Model
   - Serial Number
   - Username/Password
   - Location/Zone

3. **Copy the information** and paste it here, or create a simple list

---

## 🎯 **OPTION 2: Export to Excel/CSV**

If the PDFs have tables:

1. Open PDF in Adobe Reader or similar
2. Select the table data
3. Copy to Excel
4. Save as CSV
5. Share the CSV file

---

## 🎯 **OPTION 3: Screenshots**

Take screenshots of the key pages showing:
- Camera IP addresses
- Model numbers
- Serial numbers
- Network information

---
