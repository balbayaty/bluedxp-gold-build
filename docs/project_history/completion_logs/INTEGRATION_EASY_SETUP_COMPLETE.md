## 🚀 **HOW EASY IT IS NOW**

### **For LinkedIn:**
1. Click "Add Integration"
2. Select "LinkedIn"
3. **Enter your Client ID and Secret** (stored securely)
4. Click "Connect"
5. Authorize on LinkedIn
6. **Done!**

**No coding, no file editing - just fill in a form!**

### **For Telegram:**
1. Click "Add Integration"
2. Select "Telegram"
3. **Enter your bot token** (get it from @BotFather)
4. Click "Connect"
5. **Done!**

### **For News Sites:**
1. Click "Add Integration"
2. Select "News Site"
3. **Enter RSS feed URL**
4. Click "Connect"
5. **Done!**
