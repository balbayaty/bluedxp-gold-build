# 🚀 Compliance Module - Quick Start Guide

## Step-by-Step Guide (For Non-Programmers)

### Step 1: Access the Dashboard ✅

1. Open your application in a web browser
2. Navigate to: `http://localhost:3002/compliance` (or your app URL)
3. You'll see the **Compliance Dashboard** with:
   - Overall compliance score
   - Number of compliant vs non-compliant requirements
   - Charts and visualizations
   - Recent violations and alerts

### Step 2: Understanding the Dashboard

The dashboard shows you:

**Top Cards:**
- **Overall Compliance Score**: Your current compliance percentage (0-100%)
- **Compliant Requirements**: How many requirements you're meeting
- **Non-Compliant**: Requirements that need attention
- **At Risk**: Requirements that might become non-compliant
- **Pending Review**: Items waiting for approval

**Main Sections:**
- **Compliance by Category**: See how you're doing in each area (Data Security, Transportation, etc.)
- **Compliance by Authority**: See status for each regulatory body (TGA, MOT, Absher, etc.)
- **Recent Violations**: Critical issues that need immediate attention
- **Pending Approvals**: Recommendations waiting for your approval
- **Trends**: How your compliance is changing over time

### Step 4: What You Can Do

#### View Compliance Status
- The dashboard automatically shows your current compliance status
- Green = Good, Yellow = At Risk, Red = Needs Attention

#### Review Violations
- Click on "Recent Violations" to see what needs fixing
- Each violation shows:
  - What's wrong
  - How severe it is (Critical, High, Medium, Low)
  - When it was detected
  - What needs to be done

#### Approve Recommendations
- The AI system generates recommendations
- Review them in "Pending Approvals"
- Click "Approve" or "Reject" based on your judgment

#### Monitor Trends
- See how your compliance is improving or declining
- Use this to plan improvements
