#### **What We're Missing:**

- ❌ **Pre-built compliance templates** (ServiceNow has 100+ templates)
- ❌ **Change management integration** (ServiceNow's strength)

#### **Recommendations:**
1. **Add automated remediation workflows** - Auto-fix common issues
2. **Create compliance templates library** - Pre-built templates for ISO, SOC, GDPR
3. **Integrate with change management** - Track changes as truth events
4. **Add ITSM integration** - Connect with IT service management
