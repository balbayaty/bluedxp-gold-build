## 🚨 **CRITICAL GAPS IDENTIFIED**

### **1. Template Content Not Populated** ❌ **CRITICAL**
**Issue:** Templates are selected but their sections/content are NOT being used
**Impact:** Users select template but get generic content
**Fix Required:** Use template sections instead of hardcoded defaults

### **2. Rate Card Pricing Not Populated** ❌ **CRITICAL**
**Issue:** Rate cards are selected but pricing is NOT in proposal
**Impact:** Pricing section is empty
**Fix Required:** Generate pricing table from rate card data

### **3. Service Details Not Populated** ❌ **CRITICAL**
**Issue:** Services are selected but details are NOT in sections
**Impact:** Service sections are generic
**Fix Required:** Use actual service data from catalog

### **4. No Live Preview** ❌ **CRITICAL**
**Issue:** Can't see proposal before generating
**Impact:** Poor user experience
**Fix Required:** Add preview tab with real-time rendering

### **5. No Pricing Table** ❌ **CRITICAL**
**Issue:** No visual pricing table in proposal
**Impact:** Unprofessional, hard to understand pricing
**Fix Required:** Generate pricing table section
